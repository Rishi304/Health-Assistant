About Rishi

Rishi Kumar is a dedicated Machine Learning Engineer and a visionary in healthcare technology, with a robust foundation in developing AI-driven solutions for complex medical applications. His commitment to merging cutting-edge technology with practical healthcare needs was the genesis of the Virtual Health Assistant.

A graduate with a B.Tech in Computer Science from Graphic Era Hill University, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Rishi has consistently pushed the boundaries of medical AI. His work includes published research on innovative machine learning applications in healthcare and contributions to diagnostic algorithms.
